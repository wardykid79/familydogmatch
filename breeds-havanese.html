Havanese

Toy group · Toy · 14–16 yrs

A playful, low-shed velcro dog that's superb in flats and great with kids. Adores company and dislikes being left alone.

SizeToy
Weight3–6 kg
Lifespan14–16 yrs
Running costModerate
CoatLower-shed

✓ A great fit if…

  • ✓Flats, apartments and smaller homes.
  • ✓First-time and less experienced owners.
  • ✓Families with young children.
  • ✓Homes that need a lower-shedding coat.
  • ✓Multi-pet homes with cats or other dogs.

✕ Probably the wrong choice if…

  • ✕The dog would be left alone for most of the working day.
  • ✕You want a low-maintenance, wash-and-go coat.

Possible problems to weigh up

  • •High grooming upkeep: regular brushing plus a professional clip every 6–8 weeks.
  • •Dislikes being left alone and is prone to separation anxiety.
